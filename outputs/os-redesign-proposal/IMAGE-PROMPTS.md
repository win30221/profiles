In window exact text: 'Hi, I’m Hugo.' then 'Backend Engineer / Engineering Manager', then small quiet stats '10+ years software / 8+ years Golang / 5+ years leadership', then two clear buttons 'Explore projects' and 'View resume'. Below tiny hint 'Open an app to explore my work.' NO hero sections, no four-card dashboard, no permanent website sidebar, no giant typography. Realistic desktop spacing and window shadows. A tiny top-right wallpaper note 'PERSONAL WORKSPACE / 01'. Typography crisp, very professional, understated, polished. This is concept 01 idle desktop of a cohesive design system.
